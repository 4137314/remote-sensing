#+title: Applications of remote sensing
#+summary: Optical and radar remote sensing with automatic image analysis serve environmental monitoring, risk analysis and civil protection, climate change, natural resources, agriculture and food security, forestry, urban areas, the cryosphere, meteorology and planetary exploration; each pairs specific sensors with specific processing.

* Example
| application        | data                    | processing                |
|--------------------+-------------------------+---------------------------|
| floods             | SAR (water is dark)     | thresholds, log-ratio     |
| fires, burnt areas | MWIR, TIR, SWIR + NIR   | thresholds, NBR in time   |
| landslides         | SAR interferometry      | phase time series         |
| agriculture        | Sentinel-2 series, SAR  | NDVI phenology, crop maps |
| forestry           | optical, lidar, L/P SAR | classes, height, biomass  |
| urban areas        | VHR optical, X-band SAR | objects, CNNs, change     |
| cryosphere         | SAR, NDSI, altimetry    | snow, glacier speed, ice  |
| climate            | thermal, spectrometers  | SST, gases, sea level     |
| meteorology        | geostationary imagers   | cloud tracking            |
| planets            | radar sounders, cameras | subsurface, mapping       |

* Intuition
The same chain — acquisition, processing, recognition — answers very different questions; what changes is the choice of wavelength, revisit, resolution and algorithm for the phenomenon at hand.

* Pitfall
A product is only useful if validated against [[id:training-validation][ground truth]] in the area and season where it is used: maps transferred to new regions without validation can be confidently wrong.
