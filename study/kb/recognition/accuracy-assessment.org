#+title: Accuracy assessment and the confusion matrix
#+summary: A classified map is evaluated on independent test samples: the confusion matrix counts test pixels of reference class \(j\) labelled as class \(i\); overall accuracy is the diagonal fraction, producer's and user's accuracies measure omission and commission per class, and Cohen's kappa discounts agreement expected by chance.

* Method
Confusion matrix \(C_{ij}\) (rows: map, columns: reference), total \(n\).
- overall accuracy \(\mathrm{OA}=\sum_iC_{ii}/n\);
- producer's accuracy of class \(j\): \(C_{jj}/\sum_iC_{ij}\) (1 − omission error);
- user's accuracy of class \(i\): \(C_{ii}/\sum_jC_{ij}\) (1 − commission error);
- kappa \(\kappa=\dfrac{\mathrm{OA}-p_e}{1-p_e}\), \(p_e=\sum_i\frac{r_i\,c_i}{n^2}\) with row and column totals \(r_i,c_i\).

* Example
| map \ reference | water | forest | urban | total |
|---+---+---+---+---|
| water | 48 | 1 | 1 | 50 |
| forest | 0 | 45 | 5 | 50 |
| urban | 2 | 4 | 44 | 50 |
| total | 50 | 50 | 50 | 150 |
OA \(=137/150=91.3\%\); \(p_e=3\cdot(50\cdot50)/150^2=1/3\); \(\kappa=(0.913-0.333)/(0.667)=0.87\). Urban: producer's \(44/50=88\%\), user's \(44/50=88\%\).

* Intuition
The diagonal is what the map got right; off-diagonal cells say which classes it confuses and in which direction.

* Pitfall
Overall accuracy hides rare classes: a map that never finds the 2% of burnt area can still have 98% OA. Look at per-class accuracies, and sample test pixels independently from training areas.
