#+title: Region growing
#+summary: Starting from seed pixels or small atomic regions, each region repeatedly absorbs adjacent pixels that satisfy the homogeneity criterion with respect to it (e.g. difference from the region mean below a tolerance), until no region can grow; seeds come from user clicks, local minima of variance or a regular grid.

* Algorithm
#+CAPTION: Region growing: from seed pixels, each region repeatedly absorbs unlabelled neighbours whose value is within \(\delta\) of the region's mean.
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require image $I$, seeds $s_1,\dots,s_n$, tolerance $\delta$
\ForAll{seeds $s_r$} \State $R_r\gets\{s_r\}$; $\mu_r\gets I(s_r)$; push neighbours of $s_r$ in queue $Q_r$ \EndFor
\While{some queue is not empty}
\ForAll{$r$ with $Q_r$ not empty}
\State pop $p$ from $Q_r$
\If{$p$ unlabelled and $|I(p)-\mu_r|<\delta$}
\State add $p$ to $R_r$; update $\mu_r$; push unlabelled neighbours of $p$
\EndIf
\EndFor
\EndWhile
\State \Return regions; unlabelled pixels form leftover regions
\end{algorithmic}

* Choices
- initialisation: manual seeds, pixels with the lowest local [[id:image-statistics][variance]], or [[id:split-and-merge][atomic regions]] from a split step;
- criterion: distance from the region mean (optical), ratio to the region mean (SAR), multiband distance;
- connectivity: 4- or 8-neighbours.

* Intuition
Drop ink on a few points and let each blot spread over pixels similar to it, stopping at edges.

* Pitfall
Updating the mean as the region grows lets it drift: a slow gradient (a field darkening towards a ditch) can be absorbed step by step into one region that spans very different values.
