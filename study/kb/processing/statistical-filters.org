#+title: Statistical (rank) filters
#+summary: Nonlinear filters replace each pixel with a statistic of its window — median, mode, minimum, maximum, variance; the median removes impulse noise while preserving edges, the mode cleans classification maps, variance and range measure local texture.

* Method
For each pixel, sort the \(K\) values of the window \(W(y,x)\) and output
- *median*: middle value; an isolated outlier never wins, and a straight edge keeps its position;
- *mode*: most frequent value; used on label maps (majority filter) to remove isolated misclassified pixels;
- *min / max*: erosion / dilation in grey-level morphology;
- *variance, range, entropy*: local texture measures (see [[id:texture-features][texture]]).
Cost: \(O(K\log K)\) per pixel by sorting, \(O(K)\) with histogram updates.

* Example
#+begin_src python
  med = ndimage.median_filter(band, size=3)
  majority = ndimage.generic_filter(labels, lambda w: np.bincount(w.astype(int)).argmax(), size=3)
#+end_src
A \(3\times3\) window with values \(\{10,11,12,10,250,11,12,10,11\}\): mean 37 (outlier spread around), median 11 (outlier removed).

* Intuition
Ranking makes a filter robust: a few crazy values cannot move the middle of a sorted list.

* Pitfall
The median erodes thin lines and sharp corners narrower than half the window: roads one pixel wide disappear with a \(5\times5\) median.
