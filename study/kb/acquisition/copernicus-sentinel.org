#+title: Copernicus and the Sentinel missions
#+summary: Copernicus is the European Earth-observation programme: Sentinel missions, mostly pairs of satellites for frequent revisit, with free and open data — Sentinel-1 C-band SAR, Sentinel-2 13-band multispectral at 10–60 m, Sentinel-3 ocean and land colour, temperature and altimetry, Sentinel-4/5/5P atmospheric chemistry, Sentinel-6 sea level.

* Example
| mission        | payload                       | main uses                      |
|----------------+-------------------------------+--------------------------------|
| Sentinel-1     | C-band SAR, 5×20 m, 250 km    | InSAR, floods, ice, ships, oil |
| Sentinel-2     | MSI 13 bands, 10–60 m, 290 km | crops, forests, land cover     |
| Sentinel-3     | OLCI, SLSTR, SRAL altimeter   | ocean colour, SST, fires       |
| Sentinel-4, -5 | spectrometers                 | air quality: O₃, NO₂, SO₂      |
| Sentinel-5P    | TROPOMI                       | trace gases, daily global      |
| Sentinel-6     | radar altimeter               | sea level                      |
Sentinel-1, 2, 3, 5P and 6 were designed ad hoc; Sentinel-4 and 5 are instruments on EUMETSAT platforms. New expansion missions (CO\(_2\), hyperspectral, thermal, polar ice, L-band SAR) are in development.

* Intuition
Copernicus replaced one-off scientific missions with an operational service: guaranteed continuity, open data, and constellations planned for decades.

* Pitfall
Sentinel-2 bands have different resolutions (10, 20, 60 m): band combinations and indices need [[id:geometric-correction][resampling]] to a common grid first.
